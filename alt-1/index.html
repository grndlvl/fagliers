What We Train

Four disciplines. One mixed martial art.

Our striking, grappling and traditional programs work together as complete MMA. Mix and match, or go all-in — every class meets you where you are.

Kempo Karate

Our roots. Practical, self-defense-first karate with no-nonsense technique you can actually use — taught the way the Faglier family has for over two decades.

Muay Thai

The art of eight limbs. Sharpen your hands, elbows, knees and kicks on the pads and build serious conditioning along the way.

Kickboxing

Striking and cardio that hits hard. A high-energy way to get in shape, relieve stress and learn to throw with power and precision.

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

The ground game. Leverage, control and submissions that let a smaller, smarter grappler take over — the great equalizer in any fight.

Our Story

Run by one family. Built for yours.

What started as Faglier's Kempo Karate School has grown into one of the CSRA's most respected mixed martial arts gyms — but the heart of it has never changed. We're a family-run gym, founded on Christian principles, that's been training Augusta for more than twenty years.

Founder Jason Faglier Sr. built this place to do more than teach people how to fight. We're here to instill discipline, sharpen the mind as much as the body, and give every student — kid or adult — a place to belong.

When you walk through our doors, you're not a membership number. You're family. That's why our students keep coming back, why parents trust us with their kids, and why our gym was named a 2025 Best of Georgia Regional Winner.

"The martial arts are primarily mental more than physical."

That belief shapes everything we do. We teach respect, conduct and self-control alongside every punch, kick and takedown — because the discipline you build here is what changes your life outside the gym.

Questions

Before your first class

Do I need any experience? +

None at all. Most of our students walk in with zero background. Every class has options for total beginners, and our coaches will look after you from your very first minute on the mat.

How old does my child need to be? +

Our Little Tigers program starts at age 4. Kids 9–12 move into our Youth MMA class. We'll help you find the right fit when you visit.

What should I wear and bring? +

Comfortable workout clothes and a water bottle are perfect for your first class. We'll guide you on any gear you need as you keep training.

Do I have to fight or compete? +

Never. Plenty of our students train purely for fitness, confidence and self-defense. Competing is an option for those who want it — including a path to our fight team — but it's entirely up to you.

How do I get started? +

Message us on Facebook or Instagram, or give us a call. Tell us a little about who's training and your goals, and we'll get you booked into a class.
