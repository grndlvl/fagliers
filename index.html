Five disciplines.
One complete MMA program.

Striking, grappling and complete MMA training, grounded in our Kempo roots. Every class meets you where you are, whether you're brand new or preparing to compete.

  • 01 Mixed Martial Arts A complete blend of striking, wrestling and submissions for recreational and competitive athletes.
  • 02 Kickboxing / Muay Thai Hands, elbows, knees and kicks paired with serious conditioning and practical striking skills.
  • 03 Gi / No-Gi BJJ Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu in both gi and no-gi formats, focused on leverage, control and submissions.
  • 04 Wrestling Takedowns, control and conditioning that build a strong foundation for grappling and MMA.
  • 05 Kempo The traditional martial art at the foundation of Faglier's MMA and its coaching lineage.

Before your first class

Are the first 3 classes really free? +

Yes. New students can attend their first three classes free before choosing a membership.

Do I need any experience? +

None at all. Most of our students walk in with zero background. Every class has options for total beginners, and our coaches look after you from your very first minute on the mat.

Do I need to be in shape? +

No. You don't need to get in shape before you start — training is how you get there. Begin at your own pace, take breaks when you need them and build your strength, stamina and confidence one class at a time.

How old does my child need to be? +

Our kids classes welcome ages 4–15, with a different training focus throughout the week.

What should I wear and bring? +

Comfortable workout clothes and a water bottle are perfect for your first class. We'll guide you on any gear you need as you keep training.

Do I have to fight or compete? +

Never. Plenty of our students train purely for fitness, confidence and self-defense. Competing is an option for those who want it — including a path to our fight team — but it's entirely up to you.

How do I get started? +

Message us on Facebook or Instagram, email us, or give us a call. Tell us a little about who's training and your goals, and we'll get you booked into a class.
